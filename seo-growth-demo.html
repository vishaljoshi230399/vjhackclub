SEO PLANNING CONCEPT

Make your next
search move count.

A sample SEO workflow for a fictional local service brand. Real keyword selection must be based on the business, location, competition and current search data.

01 / DISCOVER

Keyword map

Group relevant terms by search intent: service, location, comparison and common questions.

02 / IMPROVE

On-page checks

Review page titles, meta descriptions, headings, internal links, mobile usability and image alt text.

03 / PUBLISH

Content plan

Build useful service pages and answer genuine customer questions without keyword stuffing.

Sample quality checklist

  • One clear search intent per page
  • Unique title and helpful description
  • Descriptive headings and internal links
  • Fast, accessible mobile experience
  • Track impressions, clicks and enquiries before making claims
